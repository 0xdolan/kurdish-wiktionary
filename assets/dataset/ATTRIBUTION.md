# Attribution

## Credit for the text

The dictionary text was written by the contributors of [Wîkîferheng](https://ku.wiktionary.org), the Kurdish Wiktionary, and is licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Each page's contributors are listed in its history on the live wiki, and the `pages` table gives each page's `url` and `revision_id`.

Some entries were translated or adapted from other Wiktionary editions. The wiki says so in the entry, and that note is kept in `reference_notes`.

Suggested credit:

> Text from Wîkîferheng (Kurdish Wiktionary) contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0, via the Kurdish Wiktionary dataset {{version}} ({{zim_date}} snapshot).

If you adapt the text and share the result, your version must also be under CC BY-SA 4.0 (or a compatible licence), and you must say what you changed.

## Credit for media

Audio and images are licensed **per file**, not under the dataset licence. Before reusing a file, read its row in the `media` table:

| Column | Use |
|---|---|
| `license`, `license_url` | The file's licence, from Wikimedia Commons |
| `artist` | Who to credit |
| `description_url` | The file's page on Commons |
| `license_match` | `exact`: matched by file name. `by_dimensions`: an image matched by its page and original size, so check its Commons page before relying on it |

An empty `license` means we could not confirm one. Check the source yourself, or don't reuse that file.
